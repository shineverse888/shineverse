Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all games and services published and operated by ShineVerse Global Inc.

Last Updated: March 1, 2026

At ShineVerse Global Inc. ("we", "us", or "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and safeguard information when you download, install, access, or play our games and use our services across all supported platforms.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide Directly

Account and profile information: name, email address, username.

Game data: scores, progress, achievements, levels, and game settings.

User-generated content: photos, videos, text, or other content you submit within the game (only if you grant permission).

Support requests, feedback, and survey responses.

b) Automatically Collected Information

Device information: device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers (IDFA, Android ID, etc.), IP address.

Game usage data: session length, gameplay actions, feature usage, in-app purchases.

Location: approximate geographic location derived from IP address (no precise GPS without consent).

Cookies and similar technologies to improve performance and user experience.

c) Information from Third Parties

Authorized third-party services: analytics providers, advertising networks, payment processors, cloud services, and platform providers (Apple, Google, etc.).

Such information is collected only as permitted by law and the third party's own terms.

2. How We Use Your Information

Provide, maintain, and improve game services.

Process payments and in-app purchases.

Analyze usage to fix bugs and optimize gameplay.

Deliver relevant advertising (where allowed by law and your consent).

Ensure security, prevent fraud, and enforce our terms.

Comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We may share information only in the following cases:

  • With trusted service providers (hosting, analytics, payments, support).
  • With advertising partners to deliver and measure ads.
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

4. Data Retention & Security

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, but no internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us immediately.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Access, correct, or delete your personal data.

Opt out of personalized advertising via device settings.

Manage cookie preferences in your browser.

Withdraw consent where applicable.

7. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure adequate protection for such transfers as required by applicable law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date.
